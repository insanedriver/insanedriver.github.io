What the press said

All reviews were originally published in Brazilian Portuguese. Quotes translated to English by Insane Driver; follow each link to read the full original.

Recognition

  • 2016 Roadie Crew Insane Driver nominated for Best National Album, readers' poll
  • 2016 Metal Samsara Insane Driver in "Albums of 2016, bands that will break out in 2017"
  • 2021 Metal na Lata Silicon Fortress in Best of 2021, national albums
REVIEWS::SILICON_FORTRESS

Silicon Fortress (2021)

Rebel Rock!!

Sergiomar Menezes // Nov 17, 2022

"Silicon Fortress is an excellent work, showing that Insane Driver deserves a better position in the Brazilian heavy music scene."

A track-by-track rave that says the band surpassed the promise of its debut. It praises Pozzani's clean production and Eder Franco's unique timbre, and calls "Ghosts" a power ballad and "Distant Hearts" a true ballad.

Translated from Portuguese

Read the original review on Rebel Rock!!

Morticínio Produções

Jan 13, 2022

"The band experimented more this time, blending interesting elements and delivering an above-average result in a style where it's hard to stand out."

Positive and detailed. It finds the album more introspective and more mature than the debut, reads the cover as an allegory of social media, and credits Danilo Pozzani's production. It says fans of Metallica's Black Album will headbang to "Insane Driver".

Translated from Portuguese

Read the original review on Morticínio Produções

Metal na Lata

7.5/10

Caio Siqueira Iocohama // Dec 13, 2021

"Absurd riff, excellent drums, Zakk Wylde-style harmonics, perfect vocals. The guys are really very good at what they do!"

Positive. It praises the band's identity and modern blend of heavy metal, metalcore and post-grunge, with "Insane Driver" and "Imagined Realities" as highlights. It finds that the melodic passages of "Age of Lies" and "Distant Hearts" dilute the weight.

Translated from Portuguese

Read the original review on Metal na Lata

O SubSolo

Maykon Kjellin // Nov 22, 2021

"If I had to choose the surest formula, I'd bet on what Insane Driver brought on the new album: explosion, drive, guitar riffs, good vocals and a heavy rhythm section."

Positive. It hears heavy metal with post-grunge touches and metalcore and prog influences, with more riff-driven guitars than the debut. It calls the production, mixing and mastering impeccable.

Translated from Portuguese

Read the original review on O SubSolo

Heavy And Hell

Renato Sanson // Sep 19, 2021

"Practically a lyrical dystopia that would fit very well as a soundtrack to the classic Blade Runner."

Very positive. It calls the album excellent: more melodic and more aggressive than the debut, with high-level progressive nuances. It praises new vocalist Eder Franco, the technology concept, the production and the cover art.

Translated from Portuguese

Read the original review on Heavy And Hell

Pólvora Zine

9/10

Écio Souza Diniz // Aug 21, 2021

"If you like a harmonious blend of different metal styles combining technique and feeling, Silicon Fortress will give you good times."

A very positive pre-release review that hears technical and sonic evolution and a stronger metalcore side. It highlights the opening trio, the groove of "Age of Lies", the In Flames-like "Ghosts" and the breakdowns of "Imagined Realities".

Translated from Portuguese

Read the original review on Pólvora Zine
REVIEWS::INSANE_DRIVER

Insane Driver (2016)

Pólvora Zine

8/10

Écio Souza Diniz // Mar 12, 2017

"The album's great trick is how they use excellent technique to combine melodies with stronger, heavier, more energetic passages."

Positive. It hears a modern sound that keeps classic Metallica and Pantera traits and recommends the album. Its one criticism: the gang vocals and slow-downs, as in "Faithless Breath", are sometimes a bit much.

Translated from Portuguese

Read the original review on Pólvora Zine

Heavy And Hell

Renato Sanson // Jan 19, 2017

"Musically complicated? Maybe, but with each listen the album wins you over more and you discover new elements. A fine debut and a very promising future."

Positive. It hears a hard-to-label but cohesive mix of hardcore, prog, metal and alternative metal where feeling wins over technique. It praises the modern, unartificial production and the artwork.

Translated from Portuguese

Read the original review on Heavy And Hell

Rebel Rock!!

Sergiomar Menezes // Dec 29, 2016

"A band with a brilliant future (and why not say, a brilliant present) that has everything it takes to rank among the great names of Brazilian metal."

A detailed track-by-track rave that calls the album one of the highlights of 2016. It praises the clean but heavy production, the guitar duo and the bass and drums partnership, and singles out "Change" and "Buried Thoughts".

Translated from Portuguese

Read the original review on Rebel Rock!!

Gaveta de Bagunças

9/10

Marcelo Lopes Vieira // Dec 5, 2016

"What an album! What energy! Modern heavy rock with a well-crafted sound, great riffs and sticky choruses."

Highly positive. It compares the band to Metallica, Black Label Society, Disturbed, Testament and Megadeth while stressing its own personality, and calls the album an almost-polished musical diamond. It praises the vocals, the riffs, the drums and the CD packaging.

Cangaço Rádio Rock

Cristiano Borges // Dec 2016

"Those who follow the traditional side of metal won't like it, but those who enjoy a modern, melody-filled sound will have a blast!"

Positive, with a caveat for metal traditionalists. It notes the groove, thrash and melodic passages, the Hetfield-like vocals and the professional approach, and highlights the cover art.

New Horizons Zine

João Messias Jr. // Nov 23, 2016

"Strong, explosive and urgent! That's how Insane Driver's first album can be defined."

Positive. It points to Dream Theater, Metallica, John Bush-era Anthrax and Alter Bridge as reference points and says the long songs never get tiring. It praises the production and graphic design.

Translated from Portuguese

Read the original review on New Horizons Zine

Morticínio Produções

Flávio Diniz // Nov 16, 2016

"Amateurism didn't come anywhere near this. It's a very well produced and performed album. Intelligent and experienced."

Very positive. It praises the professional production, the interplay of guitars and bass, the varied vocals and the artwork, and calls the band creative and unusual on the scene.

Translated from Portuguese

Read the original review on Morticínio Produções

Whiplash.Net

8/10

Junior Frascá // Aug 18, 2016

"With this first album, Insane Driver already shows itself to be one of the great revelations of the Brazilian scene."

Positive. It describes modern, heavy and melodic metal with traditional roots and praises Tiago Oliveira's production. It predicts the band will become a reference of contemporary Brazilian metal if it keeps to its bold path, and picks "Today Is Sunday" as the heaviest track.

Translated from Portuguese

Read the original review on Whiplash.Net

Metal Samsara

9/10

Marcos Garcia // Jun 11, 2016

"Insane Driver arrives at a good moment, standing out from the common ground and bringing something new, alive and fresh. Welcome, and please, stay!"

Highly positive. It praises the blend of traditional metal, metalcore and touches of grunge, groove and rock'n'roll, the production by Tiago Oliveira and Rua D, and the artwork. Highlights: "The Edge of Life", "Tide of Fears", "Buried Thoughts" and "Tears of Blood".

Translated from Portuguese

Read the original review on Metal Samsara

Hard'n Heavy

5 stars

Thamy Melo // Apr 15, 2016

"Has become a great promise of São Paulo metal with its excellent debut album, which surprises with its high quality coming from an extremely new band."

An enthusiastic review that hears heavy metal at the core, with measured doses of metalcore, post-grunge, hard rock and prog. It singles out the backing vocals and the rhythm section and closes by saying Brazilian metal still has a lot of gas to burn.

Roadie Crew

9/10

Leandro Coppi // Apr 2016

"Everything is done with a lot of technique, but without excess. Music with class, heaviness and subtlety."

A short, very positive review in issue #207 of Brazil's leading metal magazine. It praises the band's technique and restraint. The same year, readers nominated the album for Best National Album in the magazine's poll.
