Insane Driver (Deluxe Edition)

REL::2018
Deluxe edition // 2018

15 tracks · Released 2018

The 2018 deluxe edition of Insane Driver's 2016 debut album keeps all eleven original hard rock and heavy metal tracks. It adds deluxe versions of Firstly My Breakfast, Buried Thoughts and Today Is Sunday, plus an acoustic version of Tide of Fears.
