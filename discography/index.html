Albums.

  • ALBUM // 2021 Silicon Fortress 11 tracks Silicon Fortress is Insane Driver's second album, released in 2021. Across eleven tracks of hard rock, heavy metal and metalcore, it looks at technology, social media and modern society, from cyberbullying and fake news to abusive and long-distance relationships. Nine of its songs came out as singles before the album. OPEN RELEASE →
  • DELUXE EDITION // 2018 Insane Driver (Deluxe Edition) 15 tracks The 2018 deluxe edition of Insane Driver's 2016 debut album keeps all eleven original hard rock and heavy metal tracks. It adds deluxe versions of Firstly My Breakfast, Buried Thoughts and Today Is Sunday, plus an acoustic version of Tide of Fears. OPEN RELEASE →
  • ALBUM // 2016 Insane Driver 11 tracks Insane Driver is the band's self-titled debut album, released in 2016. Its eleven tracks mix hard rock, heavy metal and metalcore, from the instrumental opener Endless Path to songs like The Edge of Life, Buried Thoughts and Today Is Sunday. OPEN RELEASE →
