About the song

Today is Sunday is about a daily routine so driven by work that it swallows personal life. It captures the panic of waking up on a Sunday convinced it is a workday and that you are late again, until you remember what day it is and go back to sleep. The song plays it for laughs, but the pressure behind the joke is real.
