About the song

Buried Thoughts was inspired by the film Hannibal and is narrated by Mason Verger, the disfigured victim who spends years planning his revenge on Hannibal Lecter. Behind the story are the hidden wounds that bullying can leave long after the fact, and a message to reach out for help instead of carrying them alone.
