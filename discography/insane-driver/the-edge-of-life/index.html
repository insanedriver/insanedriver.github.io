About the song

The Edge of Life follows someone caught between life and death, watching their whole life pass before their eyes as something pulls them through the veil. On the other side the pain is gone, but they are torn between a body that wants to stay and a soul that wants to move on. In the end, the song suggests that the legacy we leave behind is what really matters.
