PANEL D // SONG DOSSIER

About the song

The Sun Will Rise carries a hopeful message: whatever adversity comes, things will turn out okay. It starts out in the cold, fighting the demons inside, and turns into a call to let go of the darkness and let the light back in. Like the sun, the song says, we will rise again and find our way back to the path of life.
