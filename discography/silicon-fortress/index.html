Silicon Fortress

REL::2021
Album // 2021

11 tracks · Released 2021

Silicon Fortress is Insane Driver's second album, released in 2021. Across eleven tracks of hard rock, heavy metal and metalcore, it looks at technology, social media and modern society, from cyberbullying and fake news to abusive and long-distance relationships. Nine of its songs came out as singles before the album.
