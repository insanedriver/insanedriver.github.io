About the song

Faceless Demons brings the metalcore side of Silicon Fortress to bear on cyberbullying and the anonymous haters who hide behind a screen. It speaks to anyone worn down by online abuse and the depression that can follow, and urges them to stand up to those voices instead of facing them alone.
