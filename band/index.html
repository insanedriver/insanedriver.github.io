BIO::ORIGIN

Hard Rock. Heavy Metal. Metalcore. Why not mix them all?

Born in the ABC region of São Paulo, Brazil, Insane Driver was forged from raw ideas and an uncompromising desire to create a sound that defies the ordinary.

The hooks of hard rock, the weight of heavy metal and the crushing breakdowns of metalcore meet creative arrangements and lyrics that confront the everyday — reflections on society, inner battles and the unapologetic energy of Rock N' Roll. The quintet delivers a wall of sound that is equally heavy, harmonic and dynamic.

"You need to be Insane to keep your sanity."

For fans of Alter Bridge, Killswitch Engage and Avenged Sevenfold
